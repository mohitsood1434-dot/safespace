Take action

How to report it

You don't have to figure this out alone. Follow these steps in order.

First, tell a trusted adult

A parent, teacher, school counsellor, or any grown-up you trust. Telling someone is the bravest and most important step — you are not "tattling," you are keeping yourself safe.

Keep proof

Save the evidence

If it happened online, proof helps adults and police take action. Do this before anything gets deleted.
